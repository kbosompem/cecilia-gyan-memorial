✝ Collective Tributes

Family Tribute

Read by Isaac Koranteng
+

With deep sorrow yet thankful hearts, we—the family of our beloved Cecilia Gyan from Bremen Asekuma—honor the life of a remarkable woman whose love and presence shaped our family in countless ways.

Cecilia Gyan was a woman of extraordinary warmth. She was very welcoming, opening her home and heart not only to her children but also to others in need. She lovingly took in and cared for her siblings, the late Steven Ahenkora and Vivian Gyan (Akua Akoto), showing a rare and selfless generosity that defined her life.

In our family, she was our pillar and our first point of call. Whether there was sickness, a celebration, or a time of need, she was always the one we turned to. From the joyous moments of marriages to the difficult seasons of life, Cecilia was present—offering support, wisdom, and comfort without hesitation.

She had a unique gift of connection. She did not only relate to her immediate family but extended her love to every branch of the family tree. She brought people together, nurtured relationships, and made everyone feel seen, valued, and loved. Her home was not just a place—it was a refuge of peace, unity, and care.

Though our hearts are heavy with her loss, we take comfort in the beautiful legacy she leaves behind—a legacy of love, sacrifice, unity, and strength.

"I have fought a good fight, I have finished my course, I have kept the faith." — 2 Timothy 4:7

We will forever remember her kindness, her openness, and the way she held this family together. Her impact will continue to live on in all of us.

Rest well, our beloved Cecilia Gyan. You will always remain in our hearts. Da yie.

— Signed, The Family of Bremen Asekuma and Assin Eyinabriam

Children's Tribute

From the Children
+

Today, our hearts are heavy, yet full of gratitude as we stand to honor and remember our beloved mother, Cecilia.

Mama Cecilia was more than a mother to us. She was our comfort, our teacher, our prayer warrior, and our safe place. She loved us deeply and sacrificed so much for us. Her love was not just spoken in words, but shown every day through her care, her advice, her correction, and her constant prayers over our lives.

She was always there for us in times of joy and in times of pain. When life was hard, she encouraged us. When we were weak, she prayed for us. When we needed guidance, she spoke to us with wisdom and love. She carried us in her heart every single day.

Our mother loved her children and grandchildren with everything she had. She wanted the best for all of us. She taught us to respect God, to stay humble, to care for one another, and to keep going no matter what life brings.

Though our hearts are broken because we will miss her voice, her presence, her prayers, and her love, we are thankful to God for the gift of having her as our mother. Her memory will live on in us, in the values she taught us, and in the love she gave so freely.

Mama, thank you for loving us. Thank you for praying for us. Thank you for sacrificing for us. Thank you for being the strong foundation of our family.

We will miss you deeply, but we will carry your love in our hearts forever.

Rest well, Mama Cecilia. You fought your battles, you finished your race, and you have gone to be with the Lord.

"Her children arise up, and call her blessed..." — Proverbs 31:28

Tribute from the Sons-in-Law

From the Sons-in-Law
+

Today, we stand before you with hearts that are deeply broken, yet filled with gratitude. Broken because we have lost a mother... and grateful because we were blessed to have her as our own.

She may have come into our lives as a mother-in-law... but she never treated us that way. She welcomed us with open arms, embraced us with love, called us her sons... and truly, she became our mother.

Mama was always there for us. In the moments we didn't know what to do... she advised us. In the moments we felt weak... she encouraged us. In the moments we were overwhelmed... she prayed for us. And those prayers carried us more than we can ever explain.

She loved our children with everything she had. Not just as a grandmother, but as a mother who poured her heart into them. She corrected them with love, guided them with wisdom, and covered them in prayer. Because of her, our children have known love in its purest form.

Mama was the kind of woman you could depend on. When we were not around, she held everything together. She cared for our homes as if they were her own. She gave us peace... because we knew she was there.

She was full of wisdom. She spoke into our lives. She guided us with love. And every word she spoke carried weight.

Mama was also a woman of God. Her faith was not just something she spoke about... it was something she lived every single day. She stood strong in the church, supported the work of God, and remained steadfast. She was truly a pillar.

Today, words are not enough to express what we feel. We have lost a covering, a voice of wisdom, and a source of strength. But even in this pain, we hold on to love. Because Mama loved deeply, openly, and without limits. And that love will never leave us.

Mama, we say thank you. Thank you for loving us as your sons. Thank you for standing by us. Thank you for your prayers. Your place in our lives, our homes, and our hearts can never be replaced.

We will miss you more than words can say... but we will carry you with us every day, in everything we do.

Rest well, Mama. You have fought a good fight. You have finished your race. We love you. Always.

"I have fought a good fight, I have finished my course, I have kept the faith." — 2 Timothy 4:7 (KJV)

Revival International Gospel Church

Church Tribute
+

Today, Revival International Gospel Church stands together with hearts that are heavy, yet full of gratitude. Heavy because we have lost a mother, a pillar, a woman of God, and grateful because we were blessed to have experienced her life among us.

Mama Cecilia Gyan was not just a member of this church. She was part of the foundation of what God is building here. She was a woman whose life spoke louder than words. Her faith was not just something she talked about; her faith was something she lived.

She showed us what it means to trust God in all seasons, to stand strong even through life's battles, and to serve God with humility and consistency.

Mama was a prayerful woman, a loving woman, and a woman of wisdom and strength. She carried herself with grace, spoke with purpose, and loved with sincerity. In this church, she was not just seen, she was felt. Her presence brought warmth. Her words brought encouragement. Her life brought inspiration.

Even in her later years, her love for God never faded. Her commitment never weakened. Her voice of wisdom never stopped guiding those around her. She was truly a mother to many.

Today, we do not stand here as people without hope. We know that Mama Cecilia Gyan fought a good fight, finished her race, and kept the faith. And now, she rests in the presence of the Lord she loved so deeply.

Church, this is not just a moment of sorrow. It is a moment of reflection. A reminder that life is short, that faith must be lived, and that legacy is not in what we say, but in how we live. Mama has left us a legacy: a legacy of faith, a legacy of love, and a legacy of service. It is now our responsibility to carry it forward.

Mama, we say thank you. Thank you for your prayers, your love, your example. Your seat may be empty, but your impact will never be erased. Revival International Gospel Church will miss you deeply. But heaven has gained a general.

Rest well, Mama. Until we meet again.

"Precious in the sight of the Lord is the death of his saints." — Psalm 116:15 (KJV)

Hmm... where do I even begin? Writing this tribute has been one of the hardest things I have ever had to do. Every word carries pain, love, and memories that I wish I could hold onto forever.

My mother was not just a mother to me... she was my life, my strength, my best friend.

She once told me a story that I will never forget. When I was born, I had a severe skin disease. The pain was so much that people around her told her to let me go... to give up... to allow me to die. But my mother refused. She stood strong. She fought for me. She carried me, tied me to her body, and made sure I survived.

That alone tells you the kind of woman she was. A fighter. A mother of strength. A woman of unconditional love.
